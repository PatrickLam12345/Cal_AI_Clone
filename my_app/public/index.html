Privacy Policy for Pati

Last Updated: January 2025

Pati is an AI-powered nutrition tracking application that helps you monitor your daily food intake, track nutritional goals, and maintain a healthy lifestyle.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide our nutrition tracking services:

Personal Information:

  • Account Information: Google account details when you sign in
  • Profile Data: Age, gender, height, weight, activity level
  • Health Goals: Weight goals, target nutrition values, preferred rate of weight change
  • Unit Preferences: Metric or imperial measurement systems

Nutrition Data:

  • Food Log Entries: Foods consumed, portion sizes, nutritional information
  • Photos: Images of meals and food items for scanning and analysis
  • Daily Summaries: Aggregated nutritional data and progress tracking
  • Historical Data: Long-term nutrition trends and patterns

Device Information:

  • Camera Access: To scan food items and analyze nutritional content
  • Photo Library Access: To select existing photos for food analysis

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide personalized nutrition tracking and recommendations
  • Calculate daily caloric and nutritional needs based on your profile
  • Analyze food photos using AI to identify nutritional content
  • Track your progress toward health and fitness goals
  • Generate nutrition summaries and insights
  • Improve our food recognition and analysis capabilities

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using:

  • Firebase/Google Cloud: All user data is stored on Google's secure cloud infrastructure
  • Encryption: Data is encrypted both in transit and at rest
  • Access Controls: Only you can access your personal nutrition data
  • Data Isolation: Your data is completely separate from other users

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: For authentication, data storage, and cloud functions
  • Google Sign-In: For secure account creation and login
  • USDA Food Database: For nutritional information lookup
  • OpenAI Services: For AI-powered food recognition and analysis

These services have their own privacy policies and data handling practices.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect the safety and security of our users
  • With your explicit consent
  • With service providers who help us operate the app (under strict confidentiality agreements)

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data we have collected about you
  • Update: Modify your profile information and preferences
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export: Download your nutrition data in a portable format
  • Opt-out: Disable certain features or data collection

7. Data Retention

We retain your data:

  • For as long as your account is active
  • To provide ongoing nutrition tracking services
  • Historical nutrition data is kept to show long-term trends
  • Detailed daily logs are automatically cleaned up after aggregation
  • You can request deletion of your data at any time

8. Children's Privacy

Pati is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account and all associated data:

  • In-App: Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account
  • Email Request: Send an email to trickshotmode17@gmail.com with subject "Delete My Pati Account"
  • Include: Your registered email address for verification

Upon receiving your deletion request, we will:

  • Delete your user profile and all nutrition data within 30 days
  • Remove all food logs and photos from our servers
  • Confirm deletion via email once complete

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • App: Pati Nutrition Tracker
  • Email: trickshotmode17@gmail.com
  • Support: Through the app's settings page
